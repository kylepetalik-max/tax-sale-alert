Before the list drops

  • Confirm the municipality runs under Local Government Act Part 16 (not Vancouver Charter / not rural Surveyor of Taxes)
  • Bookmark the collector / finance tax-sale page for your target city
  • Know the upset formula: delinquent + arrears + interest + current + 5% + LTO fees
  • Decide your max capital and that you can sit on it for one full year
  • Accept: you bid for a certificate + 1-year redemption wait — not keys, not possession, not a flip

When the list / ad appears

  • Pull civic addresses + PIDs from the published notice only (lists shrink before the gavel)
  • Order title search / charges for every parcel you might bid
  • Check for mortgages, liens, statutory rights-of-way, contaminated-site flags
  • Walk / drive-by if local; note occupancy, condition, access
  • Call collector: payment method on sale day, ID required, corporate bidder rules
  • Ask collector for the current prescribed redemption interest rate

Money & ID (sale morning)

  • Funds ready immediately after the hammer (method the collector published)
  • Photo ID; corporate docs + seal if bidding as a company
  • Written max bid per parcel (upset is the floor, not a bargain)
  • Arrive early — live auction in chambers

After you win a certificate

  • Keep the certificate of sale safe
  • Calendar Sep 28, 2027 redemption deadline
  • Do not take possession, rent, or renovate during redemption
  • Track accruing taxes; decide whether you will advance them
  • Talk to an insurer who understands a tax-sale certificate interest
  • If redeemed: expect statutory refund + interest — not the property

Hard rules: buy as principal · no wholesaling coaching · not legal advice · confirm statute and municipal notice yourself · no fake parcel lists.
