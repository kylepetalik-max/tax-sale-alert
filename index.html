What you need to know

When

Last Monday of September

Statute sets the sale at 10:00 a.m. on the last Monday in September at council chambers. For 2026 that is Monday, September 28. Live auction. Plan to be in the room.

Upset

Upset = arrears + fees

Floor price is delinquent + arrears + interest + current taxes/penalties + 5% + Land Title fees. It makes the municipality whole — it is not an appraisal or a discount.

Redemption

1-year redemption clock

Owner (or charge holder) can redeem within one year of sale start. You may get your money back + prescribed interest — not the property. No possession, no reno, no rent during that year.
